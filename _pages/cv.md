Scientific & Fieldwork Experience
------

**Archaeology Field Schools** - Croatia, France |  2022 - 2024
* *Pa&#353;man*, Croatia: Fieldwork involving the documentation, analysis, and interpretation of Late Antique and Medieval archaeological sites.<br>
    Site Director: [Dr. Morana Cau&#353;evic-Bully](https://chrono-environnement.univ-fcomte.fr/annuaire/morana-causevic-bully/)
 
* *Chambolle-Musigny*, France: Introductory field archaeology on 13th- and 14th-century building ruins, including co-authoring the excavation report according to standard preventive archaeology protocols.

    Site Director: [Dr. Olivia Puel](https://cv.hal.science/olivia-puel)
  
* *Bibracte*, France: Laboratory study session focused on sorting and cataloging collections from the Bibracte archaeological site.  
    Laboratory Directors: [Dr. Pierre Nouvel](https://artehis.ube.fr/membres/nouvel-pierre/) & [Dr. Anthony Denaire](https://artehis.ube.fr/membres/denaire-anthony/)
  
  
**Paleontological Excavations & Post-excavation Work** - Madagascar | 2017 - 2020
* Regularly participated in annual expeditions (University of Antananarivo & Northern Illinois University) targeting subfossil Cenozoic megafauna across the Central Highlands (*Tsaramody*, *Amporiaka*) and the Northwest coast (*Ampazony*, *Nosy Makamby*).

* Executed systematic excavations and laboratory preparation of over 120 specimens, including dental and post-cranial remains of extinct dwarf hippopotamuses, crocodiles, tortoises, lemurs, and birds.

* Documented site-specific geological contexts, such as volcanic ash layers and alluvial forest deposits.

* Investigated taphonomic processes, including volcanic asphyxiation and fluvial transport, to reconstruct extinction events and analyze the evolutionary history of Madagascar's modern fauna.

    Site Director: [Dr. Karen Samonds](https://www.sadabe.org/Samonds/Index.html)

**Archaeology Laboratory Work** - Museum of Art and Archaeology, University of Antananarivo | July 2018
* Processed sediment samples for macrobotanical analysis, sorted ceramic and osteological material, and cataloged artifacts for *[The Sacred Rice Archaeological Project](https://sacredriceproject.org/heritageatrisk/team/)*.
